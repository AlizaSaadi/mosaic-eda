Support tickets dataset cleaned from 148 to 134 documents, raising quality from 71.2 to 98.7

Data cleaning successfully processed the 148 raw support ticket documents down to 134, raising the overall quality score from 71.2 to 98.7 out of 100. Key issues addressed included removing exact and near duplicates, masking personal identifiable information found in 18 documents, and stripping confidentiality boilerplate present in 57 documents. The final dataset shows a label distribution of 53 billing documents, 47 shipping documents, and 34 technical documents with an imbalance ratio of 1.56. Additionally, centroid agreement analysis flagged 4 documents with potential label mismatches for human review.
