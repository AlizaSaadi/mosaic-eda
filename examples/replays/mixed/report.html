Shapes survey dataset requires structural alignment between table metadata and image files before training an image classifier.

The shapes_survey.zip dataset contains 38 analyzed images comprising 16 circle images, 12 square images, and 10 triangle images, with an average data quality score of 92.4 out of 100 before cleaning and 97.4 out of 100 after cleaning. While the image data scored 99.7 out of 100 with zero corrupt files or visual mislabels, the table metadata scored 95.1 out of 100 and introduced critical label disagreements, missing files, and version mismatches. Specifically, there are 3 table rows pointing to missing images, 4 unreferenced images, and 3 instances of label disagreements between the table and folder structure.
